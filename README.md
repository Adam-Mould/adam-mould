## Hi, I'm Adam

Principal Frontend Engineer at [TransitionZero](https://www.transitionzero.org), based in Milton Keynes, UK. I take products from zero to shipped.

By day I lead the frontend of [Scenario Builder](https://www.transitionzero.org/products/scenario-builder), an energy-systems modelling platform used by governments and think tanks to plan the energy transition: millions of rows in the browser, maps, charts, and an AI assistant you can model with in plain English. Evenings and weekends I build products end to end, on my own.

### Selected work

- [Scenario Builder](https://www.transitionzero.org/products/scenario-builder), now past 1,000 users. I rebuilt the frontend from the ground up and built AMP, its AI assistant.
- [A Legacy Beyond](https://www.alegacybeyond.dev), digital memorials you open by scanning an etched emblem. I'm the technical co-founder and the only engineer, and built it from the first commit to launch.
